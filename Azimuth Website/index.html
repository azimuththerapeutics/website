The approach

Psychiatry without the hand-offs

Most psychiatric care is fragmented — a prescriber for fifteen minutes, a therapist somewhere else, and no one holding the whole picture. Azimuth was built to be the opposite: one dual-credentialed clinician providing medication and therapy in the same appointment.

01

One Clinician

Prescriber and therapist in the same chair — psychiatric nurse practitioner and licensed clinical social worker. Nothing gets lost between providers.

02

Appointments That Take as Long as They Need To

No clock-watching, no fifteen-minute medication checks. Your first appointment is a real evaluation, and every visit after it gets the time your care actually requires.

03

Direct Access

Reach your clinician between visits — including 24/7 emergency advocacy and direct ED consultation when it matters most.

04

Small by Design

An intentionally limited panel prioritizing depth over volume. New clients are accepted selectively, with priority for referrals.

Services

Complete care, one bearing

Treating depression, anxiety, PTSD, ADHD, bipolar disorder, and insomnia — with the flexibility to take care beyond the office when it serves the work.

01

Integrated Psychiatry

Medication management and psychotherapy in one appointment — meds and therapy from the same clinician, so treatment stays coherent.

02

Psychotherapy

Evidence-based modalities: CBT, psychodynamic therapy, motivational interviewing, and trauma-focused approaches.

03

Experiential Therapy

When clinically appropriate, sessions extend beyond the office — trail running, skiing, hiking, fly fishing. Movement and nature as part of the work.

04

Emergency Advocacy

Direct ED consultation and continuity support during psychiatric emergencies — an experienced hospital psychiatrist in your corner.

05

Wellness & Lifestyle

Sleep, nutrition, movement, and breathwork guidance — with yoga instruction and mindfulness interventions from a 200-hour trained teacher.

06

Consultation-Liaison Psychiatry

Complex, medically entangled, and treatment-resistant presentations — informed by active hospital consultation-liaison practice.

07

Professional Services

VA nexus letters, immigration evaluations, and pre-surgical psychological clearance — written by a veteran clinician who knows what these documents need to do.

The concierge model

Why private-pay

Azimuth doesn't bill insurance. That's a deliberate choice: it's what makes unhurried appointments, integrated sessions, direct access, and an intentionally small panel possible.

Superbills are provided for every visit — most clients with out-of-network benefits recoup 40–80% of session costs.

We'll walk you through checking your out-of-network benefits before you commit to anything.
